01. The problem

Reports depend on fresh events. A synchronous request path makes customers wait for storage and downstream delivery. Move durable processing behind a queue while returning an accepted receipt immediately.

Required behavior

  • Acknowledge only after durable acceptance.
  • Deduplicate retries by event ID.
  • Keep p95 processing below 150 ms.

Failure behavior

  • Retry transient delivery failures.
  • Move exhausted retries to a dead-letter queue.
  • Expose queue age and delivery errors.

05. Rollout and recovery

  1. Shadow the new processor with synthetic traffic and verify event counts.
  2. Enable for an internal tenant, then a small customer cohort.
  3. Pause expansion if queue age exceeds 60 seconds or error rate exceeds 0.5%.
Open decision: retention window

Keep raw events for seven days initially. Revisit after measuring replay demand and storage costs with the pilot cohort.
